# Brand and naming rules

Musify has a familiar three-panel music-player layout, like many streaming apps. Its identity — name, logo, colors,
type, icons, wording and feature names — is its own. These rules keep it that way and keep the project clear of
other companies' trademarks and trade dress. They apply to code, UI texts, docs, screenshots, the repo description
and release notes.

## Never

- Use another music service's name, logo, icons, illustrations, sounds or screenshots anywhere in the app or repo,
  and never describe Musify as a "clone" or "alternative to <brand>" or imply any affiliation.
- Copy UI texts verbatim from other apps (placeholders, menu items, empty states, settings descriptions). Write our
  own wording.
- Use other services' feature or playlist brand names (for example yearly-recap brands, "Daily Mix", "Release Radar",
  "Discover Weekly", "On Repeat", "Repeat Rewind", "Time Capsule", "Smart Shuffle", "Jam", "Connect", "Canvas",
  "Liked Songs"/"Lieblingssongs").
- Use their brand colors (e.g. green accents, the purple-to-mint "liked" gradient) or proprietary fonts.

## Visual identity

- Brand color: **Glut**, the gradient from `#ffa24c` (warm orange) to `#ff3d7f` (pink red). It fills the logo and is
  the default accent (`ember` in `src/settings/accentPresets.ts`). Purple is no longer a brand color.
- Accent: Glut by default (`--accent-*` tokens), switchable to the solid colors and fixed gradients in `src/settings/accentPresets.ts`. No green accents (a test checks the hues). The accent never follows the cover; only album headers, the full-screen player and lyrics use cover colors.
- Neutrals have a slight cool tint (not the pure greys of other players). The home button never turns into a filled white house; the progress bar is always filled with the accent.
- Typeface: Figtree (bundled, OFL). Icons: Lucide (ISC).
- Logo: a record in Glut with a lowercase "m" drawn as one soft wave and two pairs of groove lines cut out of it
  (`src-tauri/icons/musify-logo.svg`). Every size shows this same mark with all four grooves; small sizes use
  versions with heavier strokes so the grooves stay visible: `musify-logo-small.svg` (30–50 px, title bar and favicon)
  and `musify-logo-tiny.svg` (16–24 px). `node scripts/icons.mjs`
  regenerates every app icon, the `.ico` and the logos in `public/` from these three files. The mark works in one
  color too (white or black, with the m and grooves cut out). No play triangle, notes, headphones or equalizer bars.
- The "Favoriten" placeholder cover uses the accent gradient.
- The yearly recap has its own visual language (per-year palettes and patterns generated by Musify), not a copy of any
  existing year-in-review campaign.
